01 / A little about me

Curious by nature.

Koubai is a creative identity exploring the worlds of code and poetry. The best ideas often begin with a simple “what if?”

I can't sing or dance, so I write code. And when an idea asks for a quieter voice, I write poetry. This little atelier is where those two sides of me meet — through experiments, poems, and notes gathered along the way.
